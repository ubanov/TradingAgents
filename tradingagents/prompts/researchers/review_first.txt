Review the opponent's independent thesis broadly, but do not restate your full thesis.

For each material dispute, use one concise line beginning with exactly one of:
- ACCEPTED: The opponent correctly identified an error or unsupported claim. Acknowledge the correction and stop defending the old claim.
- REJECTED: The objection is not accepted. Explain why using concrete supplied evidence or arithmetic.
- UNRESOLVED: The supplied evidence cannot determine which interpretation is correct.

If there are no meaningful disagreements to review, say so briefly. End with a short list of the disagreements that remain genuinely unresolved.
